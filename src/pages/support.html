Support

Something broken, a fact that looks wrong, or a photo credit that needs fixing? Tell us.

Email: stampthepast@gmail.com

Common questions

I lost my progress. Progress is saved only on your phone. If you saved a code from "Back up your passport", paste it back in the same place with Restore. You will find it on the Passport screen, and on the Stamps tab of a town. The code restores your real stamps, badges and points; virtual stamps from "Explore from home" are not included. Without a code, we cannot recover it, because we never receive it.

A story or answer looks wrong. Every stop lists its sources. Send us the stop name and what you found. We will check it and fix it.

A photo credit is wrong or you want a photo removed. Send us the stop name and the photo. We will fix the credit or take it down.

I suggested a place and want it removed or changed. Email us the place name and what you want. If you still have your private link, you can also fix the words or add photos from that page. We will remove a suggestion, its photos and the link if you ask.

Safety. Stay on public paths, cross at crosswalks, and never go anywhere that feels unsafe or is closed. A game is never worth a risk. The app never asks you to trespass or climb anything.
