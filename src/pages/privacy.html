Privacy Policy

This app is made by a mom and her teenage son. It is a game about real places. We built it to collect almost nothing.

What we collect

Nothing about you. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no ads, no cookies and no tracking. The website counts anonymous page visits, and a few anonymous event counts (a stamp earned, a first stamp, a town finished, a day played), with GoatCounter, a privacy-first counter that sets no cookies, keeps no personal profile and collects no personal data. The counts carry no name, place or location. The iPhone app does not run it. The optional test form at stampthepast.com/test asks for no name or email; what you type there is sent to us. We do not ask for your name, email or age. Location is optional: if you tap "Verify with my location (optional)", your phone checks how far you are from the landmark and earns a gold verified-visit stamp. That check happens only on your phone. Your location is never sent to us or anyone else and is not stored. The game works fine without it. We do not let you upload photos. Suggesting a place is optional and covered in its own section below. If you also turn on "Alert me near stops" in the iPhone app, iOS watches the 20 nearest landmarks and shows a notification when you arrive, even if the app is closed. This runs on your phone only and you can turn it off any time in the Menu.

If you send us a problem report

The "Something wrong?" button is optional. If you use it, we receive only what you type, the screen you were on (for example, a town and stop name), and an email address only if you choose to add one so we can reply. Please do not include personal details. We use reports only to fix mistakes. We do not use them for advertising and we do not sell them.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (stamps, points, answers to the Fact or Fiction game, and which mission you last opened) is saved only in your phone's own storage so the game can remember you. It is never sent to us. If you delete the app or clear its data, it is gone. If you use "Explore from home" on a stop you are not at, the virtual stamp you earn is also saved only on your phone. A save code in the app lets you move your real stamps yourself; virtual stamps are not part of it.

Links that leave the app

Some buttons open other websites, such as a map or a source article. Those sites have their own privacy rules, and we do not control them.

Photos

Every photo comes from a free-to-use source and shows its credit. We do not collect photos from players.Photos that players suggest are covered in the next section.

If you suggest a place

The optional "Suggest a place" page lets you tell us about a historic place. It needs no account, no email and no location.

  • What you send: the place name, town and state, a short note, and photos. You may add a nickname and a street address or cross streets. Photos are either links to free photos on Wikimedia Commons or the Library of Congress, or photos you took yourself when uploads are turned on.
  • Your private link: after you send, you get a private status link so you can see whether your suggestion was added. Anyone who has the link can see that status and add photos to that suggestion, so keep it private. We store only a scrambled copy of the secret part, so we cannot look the link up for you.
  • Nothing is public until it is reviewed. Every place and every photo waits in private storage and is checked on its own. Photos from your phone are re-saved without location data or camera details. Photos with people in them are not published.
  • Who reviews: an AI assistant (Claude) helps research the place and screens photos against written rules. The owner makes the final call on anything uncertain and can hold back any place or photo.
  • If it is added: the place text, the photos, the photo credit and the nickname (only if you gave one) become public. Photos you upload are shared under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 license (CC BY-SA 4.0) with your credit shown. You confirm you took them yourself.
  • Reports: anyone can report a published place. We count reports and keep no information about who reported. A place with 3 reports is hidden until we look at it.
  • Removal: email us and we will delete your suggestion, its photos and the private link, or take down a published place. Please include the place name.
  • Storage: suggestions and their photos are stored with our website host, Netlify.

Children

The app is made to be safe for teens and families. Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing to share, sell or delete. Parents can read about it on the app's Home screen.

Website hosting

If you use the web version, the company that hosts the website may keep standard server logs, such as an IP address and time, as most websites do. We do not use them to identify anyone.

Changes and contact

If this changes, we will update this page before the change takes effect. Questions: see the Support page.
