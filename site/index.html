How it works

Four steps. One stamp.

1

Find the spot

A clue sends you to a real landmark. Bring your own two feet.

2

Prove it

Answer a question about what is right in front of you.

3

Fact or fiction

Is the famous story solid, disputed, or a tall tale? Historians argue. Now so can you.

4

Stamp the past

Every stop adds a stamp to your passport and a Then/Now photo you can fade through. Finish a town to take its final challenge and earn a seal.

Then and now

Drag back through time.

Every stop unlocks a real old photo next to a current one. Slide between them and watch a street change. This one is the Reno Arch, 1973 to 2021.

Fact or fiction

The part nobody else does

Is that actually true?

Every story comes with claims to sort: well documented, disputed, or not true. Every answer shows its sources, so the game rewards checking, not guessing.

For parents (the grown-up section)

A game that makes your teen read a plaque. Voluntarily.

  • No accounts and no sign-up
  • Location is optional and stays on your phone
  • No photo uploads
  • No ads
  • Every story shows its sources
  • Every photo shows its credit

Made by a mom and her teenage son. 60 towns across the US so far, with more on the way.
