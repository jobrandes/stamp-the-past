    Know a historic place in your town? Tell us its name, give us a one-liner and add at least one photo (an old one, a recent one, or both), and we do the rest: the research, the story, the sources and the quiz. We aim to review suggestions within about a day. Approved places appear in the test copy of the app. No account, no email.

    Test copy only. Nothing here goes to the real app or the App Store until it has been checked. Photos must be free to use. The easy way is a link to the photo's page on Wikimedia Commons. We read the license and the credit from that page ourselves.
    What happens to what you send (test only). We keep the place name, town, your one-liner, your optional nickname, and any photo or credit you give. Photos you upload stay private until a person approves the place. If it is approved, the name, town, nickname, text and photo are shown publicly in this test copy of the app. We store no email, name or location.     "Then" photo (an old photo)

    The address should start with commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:. We check the license for you.

    "Now" photo (today)

    Add at least one photo, old or new. With only a recent photo, your place appears as a history stop. With only an old one, it waits until a recent photo turns up. With both, it becomes a full then-and-now mission.

    Or upload my own photo

    Only upload a photo you took yourself. Choosing CC0 gives it to the public domain; CC BY lets anyone use it if they credit you; CC BY-SA is the same but any copies must share alike. We show your credit with the photo. Your photo is resized in your browser and checked again on our server, which removes hidden location data. Photos of buildings and places only, please: no people, no faces, no screenshots.
