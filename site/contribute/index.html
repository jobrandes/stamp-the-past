    What happens to what you send (test only)

    We keep the place name, town, your one-liner, your optional nickname, and any photo or credit you give. Photos you upload stay private until a person approves the place. If it is approved, the name, town, nickname, text and photo are shown publicly in this test copy of the app. We store no email, name or location. To have something removed, use the Report button on the place in the app, or email stampthepast@gmail.com. This is a test and is not covered by the live app's privacy page.

    Photos

    Add 1 to 5 photos, old or new. Each photo is checked on its own before anything appears. A place with only recent photos shows up as a history stop; old photos help us show how it changed.

    Only upload photos you took yourself. Never upload a photo from a website, a book or someone else. Anyone may reuse them under CC BY-SA 4.0 (they must credit you and share copies alike). Photos are re-saved without location data, and nothing is public until a person approves it.

    Or link a free photo from Commons or the Library of Congress
    Find a photo of this place on Commons
    How do I copy the link?
    1. Tap the button. Commons opens in a new tab with a search for your place.
    2. Tap a photo you like. Check that it shows the right place.
    3. Check the license box on that page says public domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA.
    4. Copy the address from your browser's address bar and paste it below. It looks like commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:...

    No luck? Photos that are on neither Commons nor the Library of Congress can't be used yet. You can still send the place with whichever photo you have.
