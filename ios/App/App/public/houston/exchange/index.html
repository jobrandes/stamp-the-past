The story

The Houston Cotton Exchange and Board of Trade hired local architect Eugene Heiner to design a three-story building at the corner of Travis and Franklin. It was built in 1884.

In 1907 the building was remodeled and a fourth floor was added. The Houston Cotton Exchange kept using it until 1924, when it moved to a new building at Prairie and Caroline.

In 1973 John Hannah and Jesse Edmundson III bought the building and restored it. They sold it in 1983. The building is listed on the National Register of Historic Places.

Did you know? The exchange moved out in 1924.
Historians argue about this. Many people think the building has always had four floors. Wikipedia says it began with three.
