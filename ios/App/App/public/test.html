Help test Stamp the Past

Stamp the Past is a free history game. You go to real landmarks, answer a question about what is in front of you, sort fact from tall tale, and fade between an old photo and today's view. Nothing to download and no sign-up. This takes about 10 minutes.

Honest criticism is what I need. If something is confusing or boring, say so. You can stop at any step and tell me why.

Try this first

Open stampthepast.com/play in a new tab. I will not tell you where to tap, because working that out is the test.

  1. Look at the first screen for about 30 seconds. What do you think this is?
  2. Pick any town that looks interesting and try to earn your first stamp. If you are not at the landmark, tap the button that lets you check in anyway; I am testing the screens, not your location.
  3. Find your passport and see what it shows.
  4. Open any then-and-now photo and try the slider.
